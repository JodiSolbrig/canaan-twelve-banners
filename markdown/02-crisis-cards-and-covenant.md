# Crisis Modifier Cards & Covenant Meter

## Covenant Meter

**Starting value:** 8  
**Range:** 0–10  

### How it moves
- Every time a track fails (Military, Moral, or Provision), the Covenant Meter drops by 1.
- Certain Crisis cards can force extra drops or prevent drops.
- Levi’s Intercede and several Leader upgrades can raise it or protect it.

### Thresholds & Effects

| Meter | Status              | Effect |
|-------|---------------------|--------|
| 8–10  | **Covenant Strength** | No special effect. Champions gain their normal rewards. |
| 5–7   | **Warning**           | At the end of any round the meter is in this zone, every player loses 1 Loyalty **unless** at least one track was successfully completed this round. |
| 2–4   | **Judgment**          | All failed tracks now drop the Covenant Meter by **2** instead of 1. In addition, the player with the lowest Loyalty must discard 1 Goods or 1 Warrior (their choice). |
| 0–1   | **Broken Covenant**   | Immediate end-of-round check: If the meter is still at 0–1 after resolution, every player loses 2 Loyalty and the game ends after the next full round (final scoring still occurs). If the meter is raised above 1 before that, play continues. |

This keeps early pressure light and makes later failures feel heavier without complex bookkeeping.

## Crisis Modifier Deck (14 cards)

Draw **one** card at the start of each round and place it face-up as the active Crisis. It remains in effect until the end of the round, then is discarded.  
(A few tribe abilities let you peek, reorder, or replace it.)

### Mild
**1. The High Places of Baal**  
*“The people forsook the Lord and served the Baals.”*  
Any Faith spent this round counts as only half Influence on the Moral Track (round down).

**2. Midianite Swarms**  
*“They came like locusts… and left no sustenance in Israel.”*  
The Provision Track threshold is increased by 1 this round.

**3. Iron Chariots of the North**  
*“Sisera had nine hundred iron chariots…”*  
Military Track tokens cost 1 extra Warrior to place this round (or count as –1 Influence if you cannot pay).

**4. The Cry of the Oppressed**  
*“The people of Israel cried out to the Lord…”*  
The first player to become Champion of any track this round gains +1 Faith.

### Moderate
**5. Abimelech’s Ambition**  
*“He hired worthless and reckless fellows…”*  
At the end of the round, the player with the most Glory loses 1 Loyalty (ties = all tied players lose 1).

**6. The Ammonite Claim**  
*“Israel took away my land…”*  
Military and Moral Tracks both require +1 total Influence to succeed this round.

**7. Micah’s Idol**  
*“Every man did what was right in his own eyes.”*  
Players may not spend Faith on tribe unique actions this round.

**8. The Philistine Razor**  
*“The Philistines came up and encamped in Judah…”*  
Any player who becomes Military Champion must immediately lose 1 Warrior or 1 Goods.

### Escalating
**9. Jephthah’s Vow**  
*“Whatever comes out from the doors of my house…”*  
If the Moral Track fails this round, the Covenant Meter drops an extra 1. If it succeeds, the Champion gains +1 Glory.

**10. The Levite’s Concubine**  
*“Such a thing has never happened or been seen…”*  
All Loyalty losses this round are increased by 1. (Stand Firm and similar protections still work.)

**11. Civil Strife in Benjamin**  
*“There was a very great slaughter…”*  
After Champions are determined, the two players with the highest total Influence on the Military Track each lose 1 Loyalty.

**12. The Angel of the Lord**  
*“I brought you up from Egypt… but you have not obeyed my voice.”*  
Look at the top two cards of the Crisis deck. Put one on top and one on the bottom. Then raise or lower the Covenant Meter by 1 (your choice).

### Heavy
**13. The Day of Midian**  
*“The sword of the Lord and of Gideon!”*  
Military Track fails automatically unless the total Influence on it is at least double the normal threshold. If it succeeds, every player who invested at least 1 token gains +1 Glory.

**14. “In Those Days There Was No King”**  
*“…everyone did what was right in his own eyes.”*  
At the end of the round the Covenant Meter drops by 1 automatically, regardless of track results. In addition, no player may gain more than 1 Glory from Champion rewards this round.

---

### Design Notes on the Deck
- Cards 1–4 are intentionally light so the first few rounds feel manageable.
- Cards 5–8 introduce real tension without punishing the whole table too hard.
- Cards 9–14 escalate and create memorable moments while still giving clever play (especially with peek/reorder abilities) a chance to mitigate them.
- Two cards have mild positive or choice elements so the deck never feels purely adversarial.
