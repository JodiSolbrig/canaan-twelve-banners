# Setup, End-Game Scoring & Scaling

## First-Round Setup

### 1. Player Count & Tribe Selection
- 2–11 players: Each player chooses (or is randomly dealt) **one** Tribe Board.
- Epic 12 Mode: All 12 tribes are in play. Use the special 12-player seating and shared-meter rules if desired.
- Place each Tribe Board in front of its player. Give each player the matching set of Influence tokens (recommend 8–10 tokens per player in distinct colors).

### 2. Shared Components
- Place the three Track boards in the center: **Military**, **Moral**, **Provision**.
- Place the **Covenant Meter** token on space **8**.
- Shuffle the 14 Crisis Modifier cards into a face-down deck. Place it near the tracks.
- Create a general supply of resource tokens: Faith, Warriors, Goods, Loyalty, Glory.

### 4. Leader Progression
- Each player begins with their Tribe’s base abilities only.
- Leader upgrades are unlocked later (recommended trigger: first time a player becomes Champion of any track, or when they reach 3 / 6 / 9 Glory — exact unlock timing can be tuned after playtests).

### 5. Turn Order
- Randomly determine first player.
- Play proceeds clockwise.
- In Epic 12 Mode, seat players in roughly biblical geographic order around the table if desired (optional flavor).

### 6. First Round Special Rule
- Draw and reveal the top Crisis card as normal.
- No Leader upgrades or “once per game” abilities may be used on Round 1 (keeps the opening clean).

## End-Game Scoring

### Game Length
- Standard game: **6 rounds** (or until the Covenant Meter hits 0–1 and the final-round clock is triggered).
- Short game (2–4 players): 5 rounds.
- Epic 12 Mode: 7 rounds or until Broken Covenant triggers.

### Final Scoring
1. **Glory** is the primary score. Highest Glory wins.
2. **Loyalty** is the first tie-breaker (higher Loyalty wins).
3. If still tied: total remaining resources (Faith + Warriors + Goods).
4. If still tied: the player who was Champion of the most tracks during the game.
5. Final tie: shared victory (or play one extra sudden-death round if preferred).

### End-of-Game Covenant Bonus / Penalty (recommended for prototype)
- Covenant Meter 8–10 at game end: every player gains +1 Glory.
- Covenant Meter 5–7: no change.
- Covenant Meter 2–4: every player loses 1 Glory (minimum 0).
- Covenant Meter 0–1: the game already ended under Broken Covenant rules; apply the Loyalty losses and score as normal.

### Champion Tracking
Keep a simple tally (pencil & paper or extra tokens) of how many times each player became Champion. This is only needed for the rare fourth-level tie-breaker and for unlocking Leader upgrades during the game.

## Scaling Notes
- **2–3 players**: Tracks are easier to dominate; consider raising base thresholds by +1.
- **4–7 players**: Core experience — no changes needed.
- **8–11 players**: Influence tokens become tighter; players will specialize more.
- **Epic 12**: All tribes in play. Covenant Meter drops feel more dangerous. Recommend a larger shared supply of tokens and slightly longer rounds.
